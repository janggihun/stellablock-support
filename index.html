Account & Data Deletion

StellaBlock · com.janggihun.stellablock

Delete from the app

  1. Open the app
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) on the home screen
  3. Go to Account → Delete account
  4. Confirm

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you already uninstalled the app

Email us at jgh7111@gmail.com with your in-game nickname and roughly when you last played.

What is deleted

AccountSign-in identity (anonymous account or linked Google account ID)
ProfileNickname, experience points and level
Game dataScores, leaderboard entries, play session records
PreferencesSound and vibration settings

Retention

In-app deletionImmediate
Email requestWithin 30 days of verification
Data kept afterwardsNone. Backups are rotated out on a regular schedule
